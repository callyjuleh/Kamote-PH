# Kamote-PH
Kamote PH is an offline-first mobile study companion for Filipino drivers, built with Flutter. It helps new drivers prepare for the LTO written exam and gives licensed drivers a way to refresh their knowledge of Philippine traffic rules, road signs, signals, pavement markings, and right-of-way. Learners can browse study sets in a searchable library, work through lessons unit by unit, practice with questions and instant feedback, and track their streaks, weekly goals, and rules mastery on a progress dashboard. The app adapts to whether the user is a new or licensed driver, and it works in English and Filipino with a friendly design inspired by the Philippine flag colors.

At the center of the app is Dai, a local AI tutor that runs on the device without needing internet. Dai uses retrieval-augmented generation: it searches the app's own lesson content, then answers only from the passages it finds, with a source reference for every answer so learners can verify it. If the AI model is unavailable, the app falls back to showing the most relevant lesson excerpts. All lessons, progress, and chat history are stored locally in SQLite, so learners can study anywhere. Kamote PH is a study aid only, not affiliated with the LTO, and learners should always check official LTO materials when in doubt.
